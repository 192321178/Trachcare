TrachCare: A Tracheostomy Care and Monitoring App

Guided tracheostomy care at home — connecting patients, doctors and admins on one platform

Concept

TrachCare is a tracheostomy self-management app that lets patients log daily health updates — respiratory rate, heart rate, SpO₂, tube and surgery status — while doctors monitor them remotely. Doctors review daily reports, spot red flags early and manage their patient list; admins manage doctor approvals, accounts and a therapy-video library. Role-based access keeps Patient, Doctor and Admin experiences separate on a single backend.

Problem Statement

Tracheostomy patients discharged home must perform daily care — tube checks, dressing changes, suctioning — while watching for warning signs. Today this is tracked on paper: caregivers forget entries, doctors see patients only at monthly reviews, and early deterioration goes unnoticed until it becomes an emergency. There is no structured channel for a doctor to check a patient's daily status between visits.

Methods

  • Daily health report: vitals (RR, HR, SpO₂), tube status, comorbidities and lab values submitted from the patient app.
  • Doctor monitoring: patient list, daily-update review and flagged-report alerts for abnormal readings.
  • Secure onboarding: role-based login, OTP email verification, admin approval for new doctors.
  • Education library: therapy videos with thumbnails published by the admin and streamed in-app.
  • Spigotting tracker: weaning/decannulation status visible to both patient and doctor.

Solution

TrachCare replaces the paper diary with a guided daily check-in: the patient answers structured vitals questions, the doctor reviews every submission and is alerted to red flags, and the admin keeps accounts and training content current — closing the monitoring gap between hospital and home.

Business Potential

  • Hospitals & ENT departments discharging tracheostomy patients
  • Home-care and rehabilitation providers
  • Subscription model for clinics monitoring patient panels
  • Framework extends to other post-surgical home-care pathways
